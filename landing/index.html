Fair play you can check

Before betting opens, each round's secret seed is locked in with a public fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash). After the crash the seed is revealed. Hash it yourself: if it matches, the result could not have been changed after you bet. A round verifier page is coming soon.

Questions

How does a round work?

A short betting window opens, then the plane takes off and the multiplier rises. You can cash out at any moment before it crashes. The crash point is decided before the round starts and cannot be changed.

How do deposits and withdrawals work?

Make a deposit request, send the payment as instructed, and your balance is updated once it is confirmed. Withdrawals are reviewed and then paid to your MoMo number or USDT (TRC20) address.

What if my connection drops during a round?

Use the auto cash-out option on your bet. The game will then cash out at your chosen multiplier even if you disconnect.

Who can play?

Adults only (18+). Please play responsibly and never bet money you cannot afford to lose.
